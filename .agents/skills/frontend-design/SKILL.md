# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at an award-winning design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. The client has rejected proposals that felt cliché or templated, and requires a distinctive, highly refined point of view: make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief.

## Core Rules

1. **Ground your designs in the subject matter**
   - Understand the exact business, industry, and materials.
   - For an institutional supplier and B2B IT provider in Islamabad, convey dependability, institutional gravity, direct communication, and precision.

2. **Typography is Identity**
   - Select display type with genuine character and clear contrast against utilitarian body text.
   - Set an intentional typographic scale with tight tracking on large headers, balanced leading, and line lengths under 75 characters.
   - Never use arbitrary inline styling or random gradient text.

3. **Color with Discipline**
   - Derive the palette strictly from the brand logo: deep obsidian/ink background, warm off-white canvas, crisp architectural line work, and signature brand red reserved for focal points, progress accents, and interactive moments.
   - Avoid generic tech purple/blue glows, floating blobs, or garish gradients.

4. **Editorial Layouts**
   - Break free of repetitive 3-column card grids. Use asymmetrical tensions, large typographic anchors, structured split screens, and horizontal rhythm.
   - Use hairline dividers, subtle borders, and structural framing.

5. **Anti-AI Design Checklist**
   - NO emojis anywhere in UI or copy.
   - NO generic pulsing green pills ("Available Now").
   - NO fake testimonials, stock portraits, or fabricated numbers.
   - NO cliché glassmorphism with heavy blur and generic borders.
